How to read this

  • BallotTrax — voters can opt in to email, text, or voice alerts as the ballot is printed, mailed, received, and counted, confirmed by county announcements.
  • Custom system (gold diamond) — the county built its own tracker rather than using BallotTrax.
  • Florida System — confirmed to offer Florida's standard status-lookup tool; no enhanced notification service turned up in this research (it may still exist — worth checking the county site directly).
